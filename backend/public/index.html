GigVault

A work passport proved by his bank, readable by anyone.

  1. Give consent at your bank

    Your bank sends the record on the Account Aggregator rail. The platform you work for is never asked, and never told.

  2. Your bank sends the record

    Signed by the bank, fetched under the consent you just gave. Nothing here comes from you, so nothing here can be edited by you.

  3. Work out what it proves

    Tenure, weeks paid, monthly income — calculated from the bank record.

  4. Scan your Aadhaar

    Point the camera at the Secure QR on mAadhaar or your e-Aadhaar PDF. It is decoded on this device; only your name and a one-way identifier leave it. Skip it and the demo uses a stand-in identity.

  5. Confirm your employers

    We found these counterparties in your bank statement. Select the ones that are actual gig platforms you work for.

  6. Choose what to show

    You can always show less than you have earned. You can never show more.
